Coursework Showcase • 3D Animation

3D Animation Production: The Journey Behind "Kemas Bilik"

A retrospective look at producing my 3D animation assignment, "Kemas Bilik". The primary objective of this project was to master character positioning, environmental interaction, and keyframe animations within a controlled interior space.

Every asset, frame, and lighting layer was planned out inside Blender. I spent hours perfecting object constraints and smooth character transitions to ensure the comedic or daily-life timing of the room-cleaning scene felt natural.

FYP Devlog #1 • Design Phase

VRTifacts Development Diary: Ideation & Conceptual Framework

The opening entry of my Final Year Project (FYP) series for VRTifacts—a Virtual Reality application aimed at digitalizing and preserving cultural heritage. While the application is currently in its conceptual and design phase, the core groundwork has officially begun.

The current focus revolves around structuring the user experience (UX) layout for artifact interaction and establishing a clean asset pipeline. I am preparing custom 3D low-poly base meshes in Blender, which will eventually be imported and programmed within the Unity Engine once build development officially kicks off.
